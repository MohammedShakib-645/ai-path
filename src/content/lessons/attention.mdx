## What you'll learn

**Attention** is the mechanism that made modern AI possible. It lets every word in a sentence *look at* every other word and decide which ones matter right now — the core of Transformers, BERT and GPT.

Before attention, networks processed sequences step by step and forgot the beginning by the end. Attention fixes this: position 1 and position 40 can talk **directly**, in one shot.

> 💡 **Key insight:** In *"The cat sat on the mat because **it** was soft"*, what does "it" refer to? Attention learns that **it → mat** by comparing how well each word's *query* matches every other word's *key*.

## The QKV recipe

Every token produces three vectors:

- **Query (Q)** — "what am I looking for?"
- **Key (K)** — "what do I contain?"
- **Value (V)** — "what will I hand over if you pick me?"

```python
import numpy as np

def attention(Q, K, V):
    scores = Q @ K.T / np.sqrt(Q.shape[-1])   # 1) compatibility (scaled dot product)
    weights = softmax(scores)                  # 2) normalise → each row sums to 1
    return weights @ V                         # 3) mix the values by those weights
```

The division by `√d_k` keeps scores from exploding as dimensions grow — without it, softmax saturates and gradients vanish.

## Why it matters for AI

- **Self-attention** = attention inside one sentence (words ↔ words).
- **Cross-attention** = a decoder attending to an encoder's output (translation, captioning).
- Stacked attention layers → **Transformers** → BERT, GPT, diffusion models, and the LLMs powering this app.

Attention also gives you **interpretability**: the weight matrix is a free peek at what the model considers related — handy for explaining predictions.

## The full Transformer block (where attention lives)

```text
Input tokens
  → Embedding + positional encoding      (who am I, where am I)
  → Multi-Head Self-Attention            (talk to every other token)
  → Add & LayerNorm                     (residual: keep original signal)
  → Feed-Forward network                 (think about what I heard)
  → Repeat × N layers
  → Output
```

**Multi-head** runs several attentions in parallel — one head tracks grammar, another coreference, another long-range topic — then concatenates them.

## Common mistakes

> ⚠️ Forgetting **positional information**: attention alone is order-blind, so Transformers inject position embeddings explicitly. Also confusing *attention weights* (softmax, rows sum to 1) with raw *scores* (unbounded), and assuming attention is explanation — it's a clue, not proof.

## Quick recap

- Q asks, K matches, V supplies → softmax(QKᵀ/√d) · V mixes context.
- Self-attention links any two positions directly; multi-head learns several relationship types.
- Attention + residuals + FFN, stacked = Transformer — the architecture under GPT and friends.
